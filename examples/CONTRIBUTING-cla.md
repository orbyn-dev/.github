## Contributor licence agreement

Before we can merge your first pull request, you need to accept the [Orbyn Individual Contributor Licence Agreement](https://github.com/orbyn-dev/.github/blob/main/CLA.md).

- **You keep the copyright** in your work. You give us a licence to use it in Orbyn, including under other licences in the future.
- **You may edit a local copy** of the project to prepare your contribution, even where the project's own licence doesn't allow changes.
- **To accept,** comment `/orbynAgree` on a line of its own on your pull request. You do this once: it covers your earlier and later contributions, in every Orbyn repository that uses the Agreement. The `CLA` check turns green right away.
- **If your employer owns your work,** ask us for the entity version before you contribute.
